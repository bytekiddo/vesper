# Vesper — Vision

Vesper is a small town that lives on one server and grows on its own. Phase 1 gave it physics and memory. Phase 2 gives it a soul you can *see*: seasons, weather, work, festivals, faces — and a door for me to walk in.

This file is the only statement of taste. Every rubric, every "is this better?" judgement, every Director milestone is derived from it. Overseers may propose changes to this file, but only a human merges them.

## The town in 12 months

- A living town of 40–120 citizens that a stranger can watch for ten minutes and understand: who is busy, who is sad, what season it is, what is being built.
- Citizens with memory, reflection and plans (Stanford *Generative Agents*) — the town's stories come from them, not from scripts.
- The map, the jobs, the buildings and the customs have grown from what the citizens needed, and I can trace every one of them back to a decision and a hypothesis in git.
- Seasons change the ground, the light, the work and the mood. There are festivals. There is at least one tradition nobody planned.
- I can visit: walk in as a temporary citizen, talk to people, give gifts, and be remembered the next day.
- Money is still the sun. Whatever the town does, it does inside its monthly budget, publicly.

## Style

Top-down 2D pixel art in the spirit of Harvest Moon / Story of Seasons / Stardew Valley: warm, readable, unhurried. 16×16 tiles, 32×32 characters (or 32×48 if it reads better), 4-direction walk + idle, limited shared palette, soft daylight, deep but never muddy nights. Buildings have doors you can see, roofs that tell you what the building is for, and lights that turn on in the evening. Nothing glossy, nothing photoreal, no outlines heavier than one pixel.

**Style prompt (for asset generation):** "cozy top-down farming RPG pixel art, 32x32 villager, south-facing, muted earthy palette with warm highlights, soft shading, Stardew Valley / Harvest Moon spirit, clean 1px outline, transparent background"

## What makes me smile

- Watching someone finish work, walk home at dusk, and the window light come on.
- A citizen mentioning the festival in their memory the next morning.
- A new job appearing because the town actually needed it.
- The newspaper being slightly wrong about something.
- Growth I did not ask for and can still explain.

## What makes me stop watching

- Everyone doing the same thing at the same time.
- Art that changes style from one building to the next.
- Features that exist in code but never appear in a citizen's memory.
- Overseers churning for churn's sake, or shrinking the town to keep metrics green.
- Anything that needs me to press a button for the world to continue.

## Non-negotiables (kernel-enforced, unchanged from Genesis)

Runs unattended for weeks. Canonical time. Checkpoints are the fossil record. Two-tier cognition. Budget is the only lever. Content limits. Kernel frozen for overseers.
