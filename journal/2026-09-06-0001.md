# Issue #1 – The First Tide

**Vesper Calendar, Day 1** – *September 6, 2026*

---

### Arrival of the Tide‑Reader

A hush settled over the pier as the tide‑reader, Maren Selk, stepped off the weather‑worn ferry. She carried a brass compass that sang faintly with each wave, and her eyes reflected the lighthouse’s steady pulse. “I heard the beacon answer its own questions,” she said to the gathered crowd, “and I followed the echo.”

Citizens whispered that the lighthouse had begun murmuring at night—soft clicks that seemed like riddles. Old‑timer Joran, the pier keeper, recalled a dream of the light turning a shade of violet. “It felt like a promise, or a warning,” he muttered, tapping the rail where his forefathers once mended nets.

---

### What the Town is Saying

* **Lila, the bakery’s apprentice** – “If the light is speaking, maybe it wants us to listen to the sea again. I’m thinking of baking a loaf shaped like a wave for the celebration.”
* **Theo, the shipwright** – “I’ll adjust the hulls of the next boats. The tide feels different, like it’s pulling us toward something we’ve forgotten.”
* **Eira, the lighthouse keeper’s daughter** – “My mother used to say the lighthouse watches over us. Now it seems to ask us questions. I’m nervous, but also curious.”

---

### Concerns & Hopes

The council’s notes, flickering on the town’s communal board, list two primary worries:
1. **Unusual tidal patterns** – Sensors near the pier recorded a 3‑hour shift in high tide, unsettling the fishers’ schedules.
2. **Lighthouse resonance** – The light’s periodic pulse has grown louder; some fear it could interfere with the town’s communication relays.

Yet hope glimmers: many believe Maren’s arrival may decode the lighthouse’s message, turning mystery into guidance.

---

### Notices

### Letter to the Editor

*From*: **Astra, the wandering poet**
